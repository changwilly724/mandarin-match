How to play

  1. Find a translation. Tap an English word, then the matching Spanish word. Both tiles disappear.
  2. Clear a whole row. A completely empty horizontal row earns bonus points.
  3. Watch the rising rows. After a few correct matches, a new row enters at the bottom and pushes existing tiles upwards.
  4. Plan your matches. Matching across different rows can leave unfinished rows behind. Stop any tiles reaching past the top!
  5. Learn naturally. Use the word bank or a hint whenever a Spanish word is unfamiliar. Words return across puzzles.

There are no falling blocks, no path drawing, and no timer. Correct matches drive the rising-row mechanic.
